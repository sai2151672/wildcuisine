# Alex's Cuisine

### *Where Alex's Mobs Meets Farmer's Delight*

---

**Alex's Cuisine** is a Fabric mod that bridges two of the most beloved mods in the game — **Alex's Mobs** and **Farmer's Delight**. It takes the exotic wildlife of Alex's Mobs and turns it into an entire culinary experience, using Farmer's Delight's kitchen mechanics to slice, grill, simmer, and serve.

Every item in this mod has been designed from the ground up with original code, recipes, and **hand-drawn 16x16 textures**.

## How It Works

- **Furnace, Smoker & Campfire** – roast raw bison, bunfungus, kangaroo shanks, moose ribs, bunfungus drumsticks, and catfish slices
- **Cutting Board** – butcher shanks, ribs, drumsticks, and fish into smaller, cookable cuts
- **Cooking Pot** – combine your cuts with Farmer's Delight ingredients to simmer hearty stews and pasta dishes
- **Nourishment** – heartier meals grant Farmer's Delight's **Nourishment** effect, keeping your hunger bar fuller for longer

> **A word of caution:** The Maggot Salad comes with a 30-second helping of *Nausea*. You've been warned.
